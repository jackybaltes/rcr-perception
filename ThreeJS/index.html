Climbing wall controls

  • Click a hold to select it and reveal its JSON record.
  • G moves the selected hold; move over a wall or volume face to snap.
  • Edit X/Y/Z, then press Enter or left-click to finish.
  • R rotates the selected hold; enter an absolute yaw angle.
  • Shift+G toggles the wall grid; set X/Y spacing in meters.
  • X deletes the selected hold.
  • Shift+A opens the add menu for a volume, MicrobanHold, CubeHold, SidepullHold, or FingerpocketHold.
  • C resets the camera to 1 m height and 2 m from the wall.
  • Esc or right-click cancels a transform.
  • Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and right-drag to pan.
